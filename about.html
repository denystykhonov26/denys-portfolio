About Denys

My name is Denys Tykhonov. I’m a university student in Prague with an interest in web development, programming, automation, and AI-assisted solutions.

I enjoy building practical digital projects, experimenting with technologies, and developing systems that solve real problems.

My work moves between the visible interface and the quieter logic behind it: responsive websites people can navigate with confidence, and automations that organize repetitive information into something useful.

I care about clear structure, considered details, and choosing technology because it fits the problem—not because it adds complexity.
